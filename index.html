PhoneDrop

$4.99 · free trial

Send files between your Windows PC and iPhone over Wi-Fi. There's nothing to install on the iPhone.

  • PC to iPhone: drag files in, or right-click any file and choose Send to iPhone
  • iPhone to PC: photos, videos and files land in Downloads
  • Shared clipboard for links and text, in both directions
  • Stays local: files never leave your own network
Microsoft Store Buy direct, $4.99

Needs Windows 10 or 11, with the PC and iPhone on the same Wi-Fi. Privacy · Support

ShrinkDrop

$4.99 · free trial

Convert iPhone HEIC photos to JPG and shrink big videos to email size, all offline.

  • HEIC to JPG in one click, keeping camera details and dates
  • Shrink videos to fit: pick 25 MB for email or 10 MB for Discord
  • Resize photos to 4K, 2048 or 1600 px
  • Right-click any file or drop a whole folder; originals are never changed
Microsoft Store Buy direct, $4.99

Needs Windows 10 or 11. Open source under the GPL v3. Privacy · Support

$4.99 · free trial

An offline PDF editor and toolkit. Your documents never leave your PC.

  • Edit: add text, highlight, draw, sign and fill in forms
  • White-out that really removes content, plus undo for everything
  • Merge, split, compress and rotate; images to PDF and back
  • Passwords, page numbers and watermarks

InkNote

Free on the Microsoft Store

A fast, pen-first notebook that feels like writing on paper.

  • Smooth, pressure-sensitive ink for Surface pens, Wacom and XP-Pen tablets, mouse and touch
  • Lasso to move, resize and recolour; text boxes, pictures and shapes
  • Ruled, grid or dotted paper in light and dark themes
  • Export to PDF; works offline, with auto-save and daily backups
